About Augmy

Augmy is building a more human way to stay connected with family and friends.

We design technology to support real emotional understanding, not replace real human connection.

Our mission

We understand the limitations of technology and will never claim to replace real human connection. At the same time, we believe technology can make a positive difference when it is designed to support how people naturally care for each other.

Augmy is our promise to use the technology already embedded in everyday life to make online interactions feel more human, thoughtful, and emotionally honest.

Why we build

We are humans too, and much of our motivation comes from real struggles that we or the people around us have experienced. We want to solve meaningful problems in communication and well-being, not just build something that looks cool.

We also want to hear what makes it hard for you to connect with others and how Augmy can improve. The product is shaped by real needs, not abstract theories.

How we think about product quality

For us, product quality means more than polished screens. It means designing communication tools that feel safe, understandable, respectful, and genuinely useful in real relationships.

We focus on clarity, emotional sensitivity, privacy, and long-term trust because those qualities matter more than novelty in a well-being product.
