Augmy policy

Community Guidelines

Augmy is a private community for family and friends. These guidelines explain what is allowed, what is not allowed, and how we respond when content or behavior makes the space unsafe.

Zero-tolerance statement. Augmy does not allow harassment, hate speech, sexual content, threats, spam, scams, impersonation, or other objectionable material. Violating content may be removed, and abusive users may be reported, blocked, suspended, or permanently removed from the service.

Rules for participation

  1. Be respectful.
    Do not bully, harass, intimidate, shame, repeatedly contact someone after they ask you to stop, or encourage pile-ons against another person.
  2. No hate or discrimination.
    Do not post content that attacks, dehumanizes, or demeans individuals or groups based on race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, age, or other protected characteristics.
  3. No sexual or exploitative content.
    Do not share nudity, pornography, sexually explicit material, or sexualized content involving minors. Do not post intimate images of another person without clear consent.
  4. No threats or promotion of harm.
    Do not threaten violence, glorify real-world harm, encourage self-harm, or post content intended to frighten or coerce others.
  5. No spam, scams, or impersonation.
    Do not post repetitive unwanted content, misleading promotions, phishing attempts, fake giveaways, or pretend to be another person, organisation, or member of the Augmy team.
  6. Respect privacy and consent.
    Do not share private, confidential, or sensitive information about another person without permission. Respect boundaries when people choose not to engage.
  7. Use safety tools responsibly.
    Users can report objectionable content and block abusive users. Blocking immediately removes the blocked user’s content from the blocker’s experience where supported by the product.
  8. Violations can lead to enforcement.
    We may review, restrict, remove, or delete content and may suspend or terminate accounts that violate these guidelines or our Terms of Use.

Moderation and reporting

Reporting. If you see content or behavior that appears to violate these guidelines, use the in-app reporting tools. Reports help our team review content and take action when needed.

Review and action. We review reports as quickly as possible and may remove content or restrict access when we determine that a violation has occurred.

Questions and contact

If you have questions about these guidelines or need support regarding a safety issue, contact us at support@augmy.org. You should replace this email address with the exact moderation or support contact you want published on your website.
